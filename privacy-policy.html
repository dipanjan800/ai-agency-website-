Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 14, 2026

1. Privacy Policy

At Tech Sudama, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to protect it when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you explicitly provide to us through our contact form. This information may include:

  • Personal Identification Information: Full Name and Email Address (required).
  • Contact Information: Mobile Number (optional).
  • Business Details: Industry category, sub-category, and specific details or messages regarding your project goals and timeline.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for business communication and service delivery. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and requests submitted via our contact form.
  • Discuss and evaluate your project requirements.
  • Provide you with relevant services, quotes, and follow-up communications.

4. How We Share Your Information

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only when necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect our rights, or in connection with a business transition (such as a merger or acquisition).

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We will retain and use your information to the extent necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

7. Your Privacy Rights

In accordance with applicable data protection requirements in India, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us. If you wish to exercise these rights or have concerns about how we handle your data, please contact us using the information provided below.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website currently does not use tracking cookies or similar technologies for analytics or marketing purposes. Any data temporarily stored in your browser is limited to what is strictly necessary for the core functionality of the website.

9. Third-Party Services

To provide a better user experience and functionality, our website utilizes specific external assets. These include:

  • Google Fonts: For typography rendering.
  • Tailwind CSS & Lucide Icons/Devicons: For styling and visual icons.
  • Spline 3D Viewer: For rendering interactive 3D elements on our homepage.

These services may collect your IP address or technical data as part of normal web operations when loading their resources. We do not use third-party analytics or external customer relationship management (CRM) integrations to actively track or profile visitors.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please return to our main website and submit a request via our contact form.
